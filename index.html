What is PasteCard?

PasteCard is a physical NFC-powered card that lets you instantly transfer text between devices. Just tap and paste — no apps, no login, no friction.

Why PasteCard?

Tap to transfer text instantly
Works across major operating systems
No pairing or setup needed
Fast and reliable NFC interaction

Cross Platform

Works across Windows, macOS, Android, and Linux using NFC-enabled devices.

What is NFC?

Near Field Communication (NFC) is a short-range wireless technology that allows devices to exchange data when placed close together. PasteCard uses NFC to trigger fast and seamless text transfer.
